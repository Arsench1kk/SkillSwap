# PRODUCT.md

Input for design and coding agents. Facts marked `R` come from the team's three interviews (12 September 2026); `H` means hypothesis. Sections marked TEAM must be edited by humans before real use.

## What it is

SkillSwap helps a university student in Almaty or Astana who is stuck on a specific task (STEM or creative software, deadline within 14 days) get one free 45-minute session with a verified peer who already solved that kind of problem. Free barter comes first: learners can offer a skill back. A paid option is a later, unvalidated idea and is not part of this product now.

Surface type: **product** (a working tool people use under time pressure), with one short entry page. Not a marketing site.

## Audience

- **Learner.** Student, probably on a phone, arriving from a Telegram chat link, with a deadline. Already tried YouTube or an AI assistant and it did not fit the exact task (`R1`). Free matters; paid tutoring was rejected as too expensive (`R2`).
- **Mentor.** Student with a verifiable skill, asked by someone unfamiliar, probably between classes. Already helps friends for free and likes how explaining reinforces their own learning (`R2`). Needs to know what is asked, how long it takes, and that it is safe.
- **Operator.** A team member matching people by hand. Not a public audience.

## What users need to feel

- Learner: "someone competent will actually help me before my deadline, and I know when".
- Mentor: "this is small, specific, safe, and I can answer in a minute".
- Both: the session is structured, not awkward (`R3`).

## Voice

A capable older student who respects your time. Direct, warm, specific. Short sentences. Real task language, real times, real rules. Sentence case. Buttons are verbs.

## It must never feel like

- A generic startup landing page: gradient hero, "unlock your potential", feature cards in threes.
- A tutoring marketplace: price cards or "top tutor" badges. Public student profiles and search are part of the approved direction, but do not imply certification or ranking beyond evidence the team verified.
- A gamified learning app: streaks, points, confetti.
- A corporate portal or an LMS.
- Something that fakes scale: invented testimonials, counters, "join thousands of students".

TEAM: name two or three real products whose clarity you admire and one you want to avoid. Do not let an agent choose these.

## What the interface must never claim

- That matching is instant or automated. It is manual with stated time windows.
- That mentors are certified beyond what the team actually verified.
- That anything is free forever, or that payment will never exist.
- Any number of users, sessions or ratings that does not exist.

## Quality bar

- A learner finishes a request unaided in about five minutes (`H`).
- A mentor can understand a brief and respond in about a minute on a phone (`H`).
- The full workspace uses a real account; existing no-account request links remain supported until that legacy flow is intentionally retired. Works on a slow connection and a small screen.
- Honest about what happens next, with times and the time zone.

## Constraints

Mobile first (360 px). English interface for now, strings keyed for later Russian and Kazakh. Free fonts only. No third-party trackers. Contact details are never shown before a match is accepted by both sides.
